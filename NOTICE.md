# Source and additions

The retained implementation comes from [JaydenG1019/HawkEye-Data-Code](https://github.com/JaydenG1019/HawkEye-Data-Code/tree/43d083789f1badca4b040fd8fc69de0e5c3c0f36), snapshot `43d083789f1badca4b040fd8fc69de0e5c3c0f36`. The source is associated with the CVPR 2020 work Through Fog High-Resolution Imaging Using Millimeter Wave Radar; the named authors are Junfeng Guan, Sohrab Madani, Suraj Jog, Saurabh Gupta and Haitham Hassanieh.

RadarForge adds the radar_forge entry-point alias, synthetic DSP checks and documentation, maintained by nazeeh111. These additions do not establish new authorship of the research method or independently reproduce the source-reported scientific results.

[Synthesizer/COPYRIGHT.txt](Synthesizer/COPYRIGHT.txt) retains the 2018–2020 notice naming the authors and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. [LICENSE-branding](LICENSE-branding) applies only to new documentation, artwork, wrapper and checks. Dataset/CAD/media files retain their existing notices; this document does not extend a software grant to separately licensed assets. The repository contains the dataset and synthesizer, not the HawkEye cGAN training implementation or a trained reconstruction model.
